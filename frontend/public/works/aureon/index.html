01Philosophy

Beyond the structure. Into the experience.

Architecture is not an object placed on a site — it is a conversation with it. We begin with the movement of the sun, the direction of prevailing wind, the trees that were already there. The building follows.

Proportion, material and light do the quiet work. A room should feel inevitable: correctly sized, correctly lit, correctly still. That restraint is what makes a house calm to live in and expensive-looking without decoration.

03Approach

From first visit
to final joint.

Four stages, one continuous conversation. The same people who draw the concept stay through construction, so intent survives contact with reality.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We begin on site. Light, topography, prevailing wind, existing trees and the client's daily rituals are documented before a single line is drawn.

  2. 02

    Concept

    One idea, tested in section and model. We present a clear architectural proposition rather than a menu of options.

  3. 03

    Architecture

    Materials, structure and detail are resolved together. Drawings are coordinated with engineers and makers from the first iteration.

  4. 04

    Realization

    We stay on site through construction, protecting the intent of the design in the joints, the tolerances and the final surfaces.

04Materials & details

Honest surfaces,
precisely joined.

We work with a narrow palette, chosen for how it behaves at the scale of a wall and at the scale of a hand. Nothing is applied for effect.

Each material is sampled, mocked up at full size and reviewed on site before specification. Joints, tolerances and patina are part of the design, not an afterthought.
